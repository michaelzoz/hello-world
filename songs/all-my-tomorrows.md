## Keeping it both ways

Every line should fit someone who died and someone who walked away:

- Say "gone" or "since you've been gone," never "passed," "heaven," or "left me."
- Use things they left behind (a coat, a phone number, a chair, a song on the radio), not how they left.
- "Wherever you are" works for both: across town or beyond.
- Keep the regret about time, not about who was to blame.
